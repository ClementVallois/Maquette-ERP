the absence of one. Absolute dates only.

to the open rows and the checkpoint below. ADR-0120 records that decision, what left, and where it
went.

"Wave 2 plan" — that is what left. The citation is dated provenance, not a live pointer; the decision
it supports is in the ADR that cites it.
